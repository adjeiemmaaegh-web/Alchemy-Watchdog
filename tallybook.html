Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Can Tallybook work without internet?

It is designed for offline-first use. Supported local workflows can run without continuous connectivity once the necessary application files and resources are available on a compatible device.

Does Tallybook process Mobile Money transfers?

Not currently. It supports recording Mobile Money payment information; live gateway processing and provider verification have not been approved.

Is Tallybook available to buy or download?

No public commercial release has been approved. You can inquire about updates and potential future early access.

Where is my business information stored?

Core records are designed around local device/browser storage. Automatic cloud synchronization has not been approved, and owners need to maintain compatible backups.

Can Tallybook handle customer debts?

Development features cover credit sales and repayments. Advanced reconciliation and release assurance are still being tested.
