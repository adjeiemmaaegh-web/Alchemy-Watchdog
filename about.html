About Alchemy

Technology built around the people running the business.

We're building an ecosystem of practical tools that can help everyday entrepreneurs work with clearer information, fewer scattered records, and more confidence.

Our idea

A connected vision, developed responsibly.

Tallybook focuses on the day-to-day numbers. Watchdog focuses on review-worthy security activity. The larger Alchemy vision brings complementary tools under one recognizable brand.

We are in a development and verification phase. This website is a public-facing introduction, not proof of commercial availability, published certifications, or field-validated detection accuracy.

What matters to us

  • Affordable hardware and thoughtful local-first design.
  • Clear communication about what software can and cannot do.
  • Human accountability in sensitive security investigations.
  • Practical features for small and growing African businesses.
  • Secure design and careful testing before commercial launch.
