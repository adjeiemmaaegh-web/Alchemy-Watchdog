Understand Watchdog

Watchdog is a CCTV-assisted review product in development—not a proven theft detector. A review notice requires a human to inspect supporting facts.

Lesson 2 · about 5 minutes

Open the preview

Use only the trusted development build supplied by your coordinator. The preview has a camera viewer and a local Manage Stores console. This website has no public installer; never disable Windows security protections.

Lesson 3 · about 5 minutes

Create an administrator

The tested Manage Stores interface offers First admin setup and Sign in. Use a fictional operator ID, a unique password of at least 12 characters, then sign in separately. Never share credentials.

What follows first admin setup?

Lesson 4 · about 5 minutes

Create a store and grant access

After signing in, Add store requests a Store ID and Display Name. Verify with List stores. Operators should have their own accounts and authorized store access; full security acceptance remains pending.

Lesson 5 · about 5 minutes

Understand cameras and AI

Use only footage you are authorized to view. Capture and processing frame rates may differ. The local CPU text model has answered synthetic prompts, but real CCTV understanding and theft accuracy are not proven.

Lesson 6 · about 5 minutes

Review incidents responsibly

Where available, authenticated review menus include Review candidates, Review decisions and Review audit. Separate observed facts from AI suggestions and do not share footage without permission.
