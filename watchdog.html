What we're developing

More useful context around retail incidents.

Watchdog is an investigation assistant in development, not an autonomous security guard or proven shoplifting detector.

◉

Behavioral observations

Track configured zones, dwell time, movement and other observable patterns to raise review candidates. This is not a verified theft verdict.

▣

Investigation evidence

Review authorized media, event history and structured incident information through protected management workflows.

▤

Checkout review context

Use imported business-system records as additional context for investigations; live Tallybook reconciliation remains pending.

⊞

Store-specific access

Management interfaces and permissions designed around separate registered stores and authorized operators.

◷

Local AI foundations

CPU-only execution adapter exists; a verified, bundled real model and real-hardware accuracy remain pending.

♧

Customer & staff fairness

Review both customer-related and checkout-related events without labeling a person as a thief based on proximity or a score.

Features such as real-time concealment recognition, cashier-fraud detection, unattended-store notification, self-checkout theft detection and monitoring-center delivery are future goals or require field verification. This website does not represent them as operational or commercially available.

Our approach

From scattered signals to clearer action.

Designed around the way real people manage shops — with understandable information and human review at the center.

01

Capture & record

Preserve supported business transactions or CCTV observations in the appropriate product workspace.

02

Organize & review

Find relevant records, identify items for attention, and examine supporting information.

03

Decide with context

Give business owners and authorized staff more context for informed decisions, not automatic accusations.

Built for practical hardware

Local-first. CPU-focused. Human-led.

Watchdog's development hardware baseline is an affordable 64-bit Windows PC with approximately 8 GB RAM and no dedicated GPU. Real camera capacity and inference performance have not yet been certified.

  • Configurable processing rather than analyzing everything at maximum rate.
  • Camera operation prioritized over optional AI explanations.
  • Review workflows with account and store permissions.
  • Evidence and performance verification required before public release.

Release readiness

Building carefully, not overpromising.

Before release, Watchdog still requires verified CPU model inference, camera accuracy assessment, Windows security validation, real-world pilot testing, and the live Tallybook connection.
