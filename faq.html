Getting started

Can I buy Watchdog today?

No. Alchemy Watchdog is a development preview, not commercially released.

Where can I download the Windows installer?

There is no verified public installer on this website. Authorized testers should use the exact package and instructions supplied by their coordinator; do not turn off Windows security protections.

What computer do I need?

The development target is Windows 11 x64, approximately 8 GB RAM, and CPU-only operation without a dedicated GPU. Real-world capacity and stability remain under evaluation.

Where can I learn how to use it?

Visit the free six-lesson Learn Watchdog course to understand the preview's accounts, stores and monitoring workflow.

Accounts and stores

How do I create my first administrator?

In the tested Manage Stores preview, click First admin setup, choose a fictional operator ID and unique password at least 12 characters long, and then sign in.

How do I add a store?

After signing in, choose Add store, enter the Store ID and Display Name, and confirm it appears when you click List stores.

Can staff share administrator credentials?

No. Operators should use individual accounts and receive authorized store-specific grants. Full store-isolation security acceptance is still pending.

What if my password is rejected?

Read the on-screen validation message. In the tested preview, passwords shorter than 12 characters are rejected. Do not send passwords in screenshots or messages.

Camera, AI and investigations

Does Watchdog automatically detect confirmed theft?

No. A review notice or AI-generated description is not proof of misconduct. A person must examine independent facts and available context.

Does Watchdog work offline?

A CPU-only local model has completed synthetic inference tests, but end-to-end offline field performance and commercial readiness are not yet certified.

Why are frames skipped?

Camera capture and application processing can run at different rates. A visible video feed does not mean every frame was processed by AI.

Will it work with all cameras?

Camera viewing exists in the development build, but compatibility with all camera models, feeds and installation environments has not been demonstrated. Use only authorized feeds.

Where do I review activity?

Where present, the authenticated management console has Review candidates, Review decisions and Review audit tools. Their presence does not prove real-world detection accuracy.

Privacy and support

May I upload customer footage to this site?

No. This marketing site is not a secure evidence-upload service. Never share private videos, credentials or customer data through its demo contact form.

Is Tallybook connected live?

No. Live integration remains deferred. Watchdog and Tallybook are separate products within Alchemy.

Is my course progress shared with Alchemy?

No. Only completed lesson numbers are saved locally by your browser when possible. You can use Reset progress to erase them.

How do I contact support?

The website contact form opens an email draft addressed to a placeholder recipient. It is not a functioning support or registration system. Preview testers should contact their coordinator.
