Privacy notice

A transparent explanation of how this demonstration website works.

Draft notice for this static demonstration, not a substitute for legal review. Update it for the actual hosting, analytics, email handling and jurisdiction before publishing.

Information on this website

The supplied static website does not contain a server-side registration database or tracking scripts. The contact form prepares a message locally that you may copy. It does not send information to Alchemy or save submissions. Nothing is submitted to an Alchemy server through the form.

Information you choose to send

If you send an email after the email client opens, the information in that email is transmitted through your chosen email service to the recipient you address. The recipient and their provider will handle that information according to their applicable policies. The displayed email address is a placeholder; sending is not enabled.

Local learning progress

The optional Watchdog learning course stores only completed lesson numbers in browser local storage, if supported. The browser does not submit course answers or FAQ search terms to an Alchemy server. Use Reset progress on the course page to clear completion history.

External resources

The site requests Google Fonts when an internet connection is present. If you prefer a website without third-party font requests, remove the font import; system fonts will be used automatically. The site includes no analytics or advertising trackers in this package.

Security and legal review

Before launch, appoint the appropriate business contact, approve a retention policy, update the contact details, and review the privacy notice against applicable Ghanaian requirements and any other target-market laws.
